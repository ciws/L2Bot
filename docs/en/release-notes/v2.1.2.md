## v2.1.2 — Target selection and approach (day 3)

- The engine finds the nearest living hostile NPC within `TargetSearchRadius` and acquires it as the target.
- The client approaches using geodata until it reaches `TargetApproachDistance`. Moving targets trigger route recalculation.
- Dead, disappeared, or occupied targets trigger a new search. Stop cancels the route and requests movement to the hero's current position.
- The DLL reports observed combat with other creatures; occupied mobs are skipped for 15 seconds after the last observed attack.
- Fixed movement reporting: an empty path, interrupted route, disconnect, or node timeout no longer counts as successful arrival.
- Added repeatable logic checks for targeting, movement, cancellation, and engine recovery to CI.
- Added bilingual release descriptions, including descriptions for v2.1.0 and v2.1.1.

### Settings and current scope

`config.json`: `TargetSearchRadius` defaults to **1500**, `TargetApproachDistance` to **80** world units. Restart the client after changing settings.

This release selects and approaches mobs, then waits in `Fight`. Automatic attacks, looting, and recovery remain later roadmap steps. Combat detection depends on observed attack packets; an unseen fight or a gap of 15 seconds can make an occupied mob appear available. Live Windows/game validation is still required.

### Download

Download `L2Bot-win-x86.zip` under **Assets**, extract it, and run `Client.exe` on Windows. The archive includes the .NET 8 WPF client, bot DLL, native pathfinder, and 165 geodata regions.
